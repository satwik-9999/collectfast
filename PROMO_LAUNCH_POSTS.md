**Body**:
Hey everyone,

Like most freelancers and agency folks in India, I noticed two huge problems when dealing with client payments:
1. Standard payment gateways (Razorpay, Stripe) take 2% to 3% + 18% GST on every single invoice. On a ₹50,000 invoice, you're literally throwing away ₹1,000–₹1,500.
2. Invoices sit unpaid for 30–45 days because no one likes making awkward phone calls to ask for money.

I decided to solve both by building **CollectFast** using Python, FastAPI, and Tailwind:

* **1-Click WhatsApp Chaser**: Generates 3 behavioral nudges (Friendly Nudge, Due Date Notice, Overdue Warning) that open WhatsApp with the payment link pre-filled.
* **Automated PDF Generator**: Fast server-side tax invoice PDF generation with embedded QR codes.
